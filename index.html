ARCHITECTURE

Kubernetes-native by design.

Kaniop continuously reconciles Kubernetes custom resources. Kubernetes defines the desired deployment state, Kaniop coordinates operations, and Kanidm remains responsible for identity, persistence, and replication correctness.
